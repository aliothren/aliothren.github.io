I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Arizona, advised by **[Prof. Huanrui Yang](https://sites.google.com/view/huanrui-yang)**.

My research focuses on efficient and reliable AI, spanning model design, compression, and hardware-aware inference. I study how neural networks can be simplified while preserving their capabilities, how LLM agents can remain effective under perturbations and resource constraints, and how computation and communication shape inference performance on emerging hardware.

Before starting my Ph.D., I completed the EIT Digital dual-degree master's program in Autonomous Systems at KTH Royal Institute of Technology and EURECOM. I received my bachelor's degree in Automation from Xi'an Jiaotong University. I have also held internships at TetraMem, Synkrotron, and Siemens.
